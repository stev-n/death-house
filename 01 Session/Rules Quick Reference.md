> [!lore] The dozen rules you actually look up mid-session. Everything else, rule fast and move on.

## Death & Dying

- **0 HP:** unconscious, drop prone, start making death saves on your turn
- **Death save:** d20, no modifiers. 10+ succeeds. Three successes = stable. Three failures = dead.
- **Nat 20:** regain 1 HP and wake up. **Nat 1:** counts as two failures.
- **Damage while at 0:** one automatic failure. A crit is two.
- **Massive damage:** damage remaining after dropping to 0 that equals your HP maximum kills outright.
- **Stabilised:** regain 1 HP after 1d4 hours.

## Conditions That Come Up Here

| Condition | The part you'll forget |
|---|---|
| **Frightened** | can't willingly move *closer* to the source; disadvantage while it's in sight |
| **Grappled** | speed 0, ends if the grappler is incapacitated |
| **Paralysed** | can't act; attacks within 5 ft auto-crit; auto-fails STR and DEX saves |
| **Poisoned** | disadvantage on attacks *and* ability checks |
| **Prone** | melee attacks against you have advantage, ranged have disadvantage |
| **Restrained** | speed 0, attacks against you have advantage, disadvantage on DEX saves |
| **Incapacitated** | no actions, no reactions |
| **Unconscious** | incapacitated + prone + drops everything + auto-fails STR/DEX saves |

## Light & Darkness

- **Bright light:** see normally
- **Dim light:** lightly obscured — disadvantage on sight-based Perception
- **Darkness:** heavily obscured — effectively blinded
- **Darkvision:** darkness counts as dim light, dim as bright. **You still can't read in it, and everything is greyscale.**
- **Torch:** 20 ft bright, 20 ft dim, 1 hour. **Lantern:** 30/30, 6 hours. **Light cantrip:** 20/20.

## Hiding & Perception

- **Passive Perception** = 10 + WIS(Perception) modifier. Use it for anything the party isn't actively searching for.
- You can't hide from something that can see you clearly.
- **Searching a 10 ft square** takes about a minute.

## Common Actions

Attack · Cast a Spell · Dash · Disengage · Dodge · Help · Hide · Ready · Search · Use an Object

- **Dodge:** attacks against you have disadvantage; you have advantage on DEX saves
- **Help:** give an ally advantage on one check, or on one attack against a creature within 5 ft of you
- **Ready:** pick a trigger and a response; costs your reaction when it fires

## Advantage & Disadvantage

Roll twice, take the better or worse. **They don't stack** — any number of each cancels to a flat roll.

## Cover

| Cover | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Half | +2 AC and DEX saves |
| Three-quarters | +5 AC and DEX saves |
| Total | can't be targeted directly |

## Resting

- **Short rest:** 1 hour, spend Hit Dice to heal
- **Long rest:** 8 hours, regain all HP and half your total Hit Dice
- Interrupted by an hour of walking, fighting, or casting? It doesn't count.

> [!check] In this house
> Decide *before* the session whether a long rest is possible inside, and say so when they ask.
