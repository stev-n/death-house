shaft in the attic drops 50 feet straight to area 22.

## Map Notes

> [!check]- If the pins look vertically flipped
> Leaflet reads latitude from the **bottom** of the image. If every pin lands mirrored,
> open the floor note and replace each marker's latitude `N` with `100 − N`.
> Easier: just drag them — Leaflet saves the new positions.

> [!lore]- Other lighting sets are available
> The vault ships the **Gloom** set (lit + lights-out). The full pack in your Downloads
> also has *Night*, *Dark Fairytale*, *Ghostly*, *Warm*, and *Sunny Day*, each with
> Fog and Rain variants, in 4K and 8K, gridded and ungridded. Drop any of them into
> `06 Assets/Maps` and point the floor note's `image:` line at the new file.
